Quick Reference
Kraken Servers — Add one entry per KrakenSDR. Click Configure to set each station's DOA exclusion range, persist mode, and line length independently.
TAK Destination — IPv4 and port of your TAK Server input. Multicast additionally broadcasts to 239.2.3.1:6969 for direct client reception.
DOA Exclusion — Bearings inside the shaded wedge are suppressed for that station. Useful when a known transmitter is in a fixed direction.
Persist Lines — Off: one line per station updated each cycle. On: each bearing generates a new line, leaving a history of readings.
Line Length — Length of the DOA bearing line in TAK, in kilometers. Configurable per station.
